================================================
  VISITING CARD SCANNER  —  KAISE USE KAREIN
        (100% FREE — koi kharcha nahi)
================================================

Ye app aapke visiting cards ke photo ya PDF se data nikaal ke ek list
me daal deta hai, jise aap Excel me export kar sakte ho.
Card padhna aapke PHONE ke andar hi hota hai (on-device OCR) —
koi paisa nahi, koi login nahi, koi API key nahi.

Sabse accurate: ENGLISH cards. (Gujarati/Hindi optional — kam accurate.)


------------------------------------------------
PEHLI BAAR
------------------------------------------------
Pehli baar app khulte waqt OCR engine (~11MB) download hota hai.
Uske baad app OFFLINE bhi chalta hai — internet ki zaroorat nahi.


------------------------------------------------
STEP 1 — CARD ADD KARO  ("Scan" tab)
------------------------------------------------
2 tareeke:

A) PDF:  "File / Photo" -> apni PDF chuno.
   - Ek PDF me BAHUT saare card ho sakte hain.
   - Upar switch dekho: "Har card ke 2 side (aage+piche)?"
       OFF = PDF ka har 1 page  = 1 card (sirf aage)
       ON  = PDF ke har 2 page  = 1 card (aage + piche)

B) Photo:  "Camera" se khich lo, ya "File / Photo" se gallery se
   ek saath kai photo chuno. Wahi switch yahaan bhi lagta hai
   (ON = har 2 photo 1 card, OFF = har 1 photo 1 card).

Phir app ek-ek card padh ke list me daal dega (progress dikhega).


------------------------------------------------
EK PDF ME KITNE CARD DAALU? (zaroori)
------------------------------------------------
- Behtar: ek PDF me ~25 se 50 card (sirf aage waale).
          Aage+piche waale ho to ek PDF me ~15 se 25 card.
- PDF ka size ~40-50 MB se kam rakho.
- Har card padhne me phone pe ~1-3 second lagta hai, isliye 200-300
  card ki ek hi PDF me kaafi time lagega. Chhoti-chhoti PDF me baato.
- Ginti ki koi hard limit nahi — bas badi PDF dheemi + zyada memory
  leti hai. Multiple PDF ek-ek karke daalna zyada aasaan rehta hai.
- Tip: PDF banane ke chakkar me na pado to seedha gallery se 20-30
  photo ek saath select karna sabse fast hai.


------------------------------------------------
STEP 2 — CHECK / EDIT  ("Cards" tab)
------------------------------------------------
- Har card pe tap karo -> saari details edit kar sakte ho.
- Neeche "Poora scan text" box me app ne jo padha wo dikhta hai +
  upar card ki photo -> dekh ke jaldi se theek kar lo.
- Search box se naam/phone/company dhundo.
- Category se filter (Doctor / Pharma / Medical / Hospital ...).
- 📞 button se seedha call.


------------------------------------------------
CALL DAY / CALL TIMING (pen se likhi note)
------------------------------------------------
Card pe aapne pen se jo call ke din/time likhe hain, app unhe bhi
padhne ki koshish karta hai:
   M / Mon = Monday      Th / Thu = Thursday
   T / Tue = Tuesday     F  / Fri = Friday
   W / Wed = Wednesday   S  / Sat = Saturday
   Time jaise 11-1, 5 to 8, 10:30-1:30

ZAROORI: Handwriting (haath ki likhaawat) OCR se HAMESHA sahi nahi
padhti. Isliye Call Day / Call Timing ko ek "guess" samjho —
edit screen me card ki photo dekh ke 2 second me theek/confirm kar lo.
Ye dono Excel me alag column me bhi aate hain.


------------------------------------------------
STEP 3 — EXCEL EXPORT  ("Export" tab)
------------------------------------------------
- "Excel (.xlsx)" -> saara data (Call Day/Timing sameet) ek file me.
- "CSV" bhi le sakte ho.
- "Backup (.json)" -> photo + data ka pura backup. Har kuch din me lo.
  Naye phone pe "Restore" se wapas aa jaata hai.


------------------------------------------------
BEHTAR RESULT KE TIPS
------------------------------------------------
- Achhi roshni, seedha (tirchha nahi), poora card frame me -> zyada sahi.
- Pen note saaf aur bade akshar me ho to behtar padhega.
- Ek baar 20-30 card se shuru karo, sab theek lage to zyada karo.
- English cards sabse accurate. Gujarati/Hindi card ho to "Settings"
  me us language ka switch ON karo (thoda dheema hoga).
